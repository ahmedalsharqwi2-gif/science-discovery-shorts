## What the model represents

Oil and water meet at an interface. A single surfactant shows a spherical hydrophilic head and a nonpolar tail. The micelle places heads toward surrounding water and tails toward the oil. The rinse carries the assembly across a surface; the 20-tick timer supports the handwashing instruction. The comparison leaves some illustrative markers after cleaning, avoiding an assertion of complete sterilization. These are explanatory drawings, not microscopy, measured molecular sizes or a molecular dynamics simulation. The on-screen scale disclaimer remains visible.
