# Professional daily factory — science

One supported production workflow: `.github/workflows/professional-daily.yml`. Each successful film chooses a different sourced subject, creates eight authored 3D scenes, produces original Charon narration, measures phrase timing, composes music, verifies the final video, and reserves the subject durably in `state/professional_topics.json`. A new title for an old topic is still a repeat. If no new suitable evidence-backed subject is available, production stops rather than recycling a film or inventing facts.

Review mode never posts externally. The user approved the channel samples and daily publication on 2026-10-29; config/professional_release.json pins the approved complete sample checksum. Source, novelty, complete speech and 90–180-second technical checks remain required; cosmetic preferences are advisory.

UTC cron runs at 06:00 and 07:00; an Africa/Cairo civil-time guard selects local 09:00 exactly once across summer and winter. It prepares a new film and submits an absolute 17:00 Cairo scheduled publication to verified configured channels. A late film never falls back to immediate posting. Provider acceptance receipts and topic reservations prevent duplicate delivery. The current explicitly authorized one-off completed films may be published immediately through the separate immutable grant.

Future horror films use documented incidents with escalating suspense; automatic generic fictional loops are disabled. AI news combines significant recent developments and topical Egyptian satire, with at most 36-hour-old candidates and a hard 48-hour check at the publication time. Music remains -27 LUFS, captions clear of bottom platform UI, and original Charon narration is retained.

Videos and diagnostics are Actions artifacts. Review voice and safe metadata are also delivered to a run-specific review branch for Git-based inspection; secrets are never copied there. Media downloads for the user belong on separate review-media branches, not main. No publication is confirmed without real provider post/video IDs.
